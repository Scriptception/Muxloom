# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Muxloom welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and keep disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where practical.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
